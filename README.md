# T-Minus Countdown: Job Preparation

A neon cyberpunk Android lock screen wallpaper and preparation tracker built around a **December 15, 2026** deadline.

## Included Files
- `wallpaper_lockscreen_dec15_2026.jpg` — 9:16 portrait lock screen image with top clock clearance, countdown readout, checkboxes, milestones, and motivational quote.
- `milestones.json` — Structured deadline, countdown reference, and milestone checklist data.

## Design & Layout
- **Style**: Neon Cyberpunk
- **Palette**: Matte black background with electric purple, glowing blue/cyan, and neon pink accents
- **Format**: 9:16 Android portrait lock screen with open top area for clock and notifications
- **Title**: `T-MINUS COUNTDOWN: JOB PREPARATION`
- **Reference Date**: November 25, 2026 (`20 DAYS LEFT` to December 15, 2026)

## Milestones
- `[ ]` **T-14 (December 1, 2026)**: Revise Java and SQL.
- `[ ]` **T-7 (December 8, 2026)**: Practice coding and aptitude.
- `[ ]` **T-3 (December 12, 2026)**: Take mock interviews.
- `[ ]` **T-1 (December 14, 2026)**: Final revision and rest.

## How to Use
1. Transfer `wallpaper_lockscreen_dec15_2026.jpg` to your Android device.
2. Open **Settings > Wallpaper & style** (or **Wallpaper**), select the image, and set it to **Lock screen**.
3. Check off milestones as you complete each stage and regenerate or update the day count as needed.
